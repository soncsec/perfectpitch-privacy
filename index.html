Perfect Pitch: Daily Note — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Perfect Pitch: Daily Note ("the app") is a daily ear-training game. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What we don't collect

The app has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, microphone, or photos. The game doesn't save your play on your device. It remembers one setting, the reference note you pick for Easy mode, on your device only; it is never sent anywhere. The analytics service below keeps only a small temporary cache of anonymous events until they are sent.

Anonymous gameplay analytics

To improve the game, the app sends a few anonymous events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service:

EventWhat it includes
A daily game started, finished, or was quitEasy or Hard; which question you quit on; a rough time range (for example "30–44 seconds"); number of wrong answers and replays; whether the game was ranked
"I can't hear anything" on the sound checkEasy or Hard

TelemetryDeck also receives standard technical details: app and iOS version, device model, language, and a random identifier that is hashed so it can't be traced back to you. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising.

Game Center

Leaderboards are optional and use Apple's Game Center. If you're signed in, the app sends your daily time to Apple and shows your rank. Apple handles your Game Center account under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive or store your Game Center details anywhere else.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
