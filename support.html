Perfect Pitch: Daily Note — Support

Last updated: October 24, 2026

How to play

Every day the app plays ten notes. Pick each note from six choices as fast as you can. A wrong answer adds 10 seconds and shows the right note. Easy plays a C first as a reference and uses 7 notes; Hard uses all 12 notes with no reference. New puzzles start at midnight Eastern time.

Common questions

I can't hear anything. Turn up the volume and check that Bluetooth headphones are connected. The game plays sound even with the Silent switch on.

Why didn't my second game count on the leaderboard? Only your first finished game each day is ranked. Play again as often as you like for practice; those games show "Replay".

Why does it say "Not ranked"? Leaderboards need Game Center. Sign in under Settings → Game Center, or tap "Sign in" on the score screen. If you finished just after midnight Eastern, the new day's board has started and that game isn't ranked.

Does the app save my scores? No. Your daily rank lives in Game Center. See the privacy policy.
